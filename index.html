    Questions

    Straight answers

    Are you really available 24/7?
    Yes. Junk Voyage runs on-demand around the clock, including nights, weekends and holidays. If you call at 3 AM because a tenant left a unit full of furniture and the new one moves in at nine, that is exactly the call we built the business for.
    How much does junk removal cost?
    Residential jobs are priced by how much of our 14 ft dump trailer you fill, starting at a $150 minimum pickup and running to $800 for a full load. The full sheet is published on our pricing page — labor, hauling and dump fees are already included. Commercial and construction jobs are quoted on site, still free and still upfront.
    Do I need to move anything to the curb?
    No. That is the point. We come inside, up the stairs, into the basement or out to the back shed, and we carry it out ourselves. You wave at it, we take it.
    Can you come the same day?
    Usually yes, often within a few hours. Same-day and next-day slots are our normal operation rather than an upcharge. Call and we will tell you honestly what time we can be there.
    What happens to my stuff?
    Anything still usable goes to donation, and metal, appliances and electronics go to recycling. Only what is genuinely finished ends up at the transfer station.
    Do you work with landlords, realtors and contractors?
    Constantly. Property managers, investors, realtors prepping a listing, and contractors who need debris cleared mid-project are a big part of our week. Ask about recurring and multi-property scheduling.
    How do I get an estimate?
    Call or text 612-465-9587, or send the form on this page. A couple of phone photos are usually enough for us to give you a number before we ever roll out. Estimates are always free and there is no obligation.
