What this job looks like

Need it gone today? Need it gone tonight? Even at 3 AM? Junk Voyage is available around the clock to provide fast, reliable junk removal whenever you need it.

Most haulers give you a three-day window and a two-week wait. We built this business around the opposite: you call, we tell you a real time, and the junk is gone. Same-day and next-day are our normal operation, not a rush upcharge, and there is no premium added for evenings, weekends or holidays.

  • Book and haul the same dayOften within a couple of hours of your call.
  • Evening, overnight and weekend slotsIncluding holidays. The phone is on.
  • One item or a full loadA single mattress is just as welcome as a whole basement.
  • A real time, not a windowWe tell you when we will be there and we show up then.
