Residential Junk Removal

Old furniture, appliances, household clutter and the pile that has been “getting sorted” for three years. We clear the space and leave it swept.

Call 612-465-9587 Get a free estimate

For homeowners and renters

What this job looks like

From old furniture and appliances to household clutter and unwanted items, we make it easy to clear your space. Our team handles all the lifting, loading and hauling so you do not have to.

You do not need to drag anything to the curb, take anything apart, or borrow a truck. Point at what is going and step back — we come inside, up the stairs, down to the basement or out to the back shed and carry it out ourselves.

  • Whole house or one roomPriced the same way either way, off the volume sheet.
  • Nothing gets damagedDoorframes, walls, floors and driveways are protected as we work.
  • Swept when we finishYou get a usable space back, not an empty room with dust in it.
  • Donated and recycled firstUsable furniture and appliances get diverted before the landfill.

Priced from our published sheet

Residential jobs are priced by how much of our 14 ft dump trailer you fill — a $120 minimum pickup up to $699 for a full load, with labor, hauling and dump fees included. Commercial, construction and extra-heavy loads are quoted on site, still free and still upfront.
