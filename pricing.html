No surprises

What actually changes the price

Three things, and you will know all of them before we lift anything.

  • How much space it takes upVolume in the trailer is the main number. That is the sheet above.
  • How heavy the material isConcrete, brick, dirt, shingles and tile weigh far more than furniture and cost more to dump, so heavy loads get their own quote.
  • Whether anything is regulatedPaint, chemicals, asbestos, propane and similar have to go to a licensed facility. We will tell you where — free.

Everything else — stairs, distance to the truck, disassembly, evening or overnight timing — is included. We do not price-penalize a hard job.

Pricing questions

Straight answers

Is the estimate really free?
Yes, always, with no obligation to book. Send a photo or two and we can usually quote you over the phone in a few minutes.
Can the price go up once you arrive?
Only if what is actually there is bigger than what was described — and in that case we tell you the new number and wait for your yes before anything moves. We never revise a price after the work is done.
Do you charge extra for stairs or a long carry?
No. Stairs, basements, back yards, disassembly and awkward doorways are part of the job, not a line item.
Is there an extra charge for nights or weekends?
Being available around the clock is the service, not a premium tier. Evening, overnight and weekend jobs are priced from the same sheet.
What if I have more than a full trailer?
We do multiple trips. Tell us the whole scope and we will price the full clear-out up front so you know the total, not just the first load.
How do I pay?
Payment is due when the job is finished and you are happy with the space. Ask us which methods we take when you book.
